FOR SMALL IMPORTERS WITHOUT A TEAM IN CHINA

## Buying from China is easier when someone can follow up locally

You may have found a supplier on Alibaba.com and agreed a price, but still feel unsure about paying. Or you may have placed an order through Made-in-China.com and discovered that packaging, accessories and shipping documents remain unresolved. Without a team in China, one person often has to manage the supplier, interpret the paperwork and arrange freight—without seeing the goods.

**TengYoda is here to help you understand the problem before you decide what to do next.** Initial consultation is free. Ask us about supplier checks, inspection arrangements, collection, warehousing, consolidation or shipping coordination. You do not need to be ready to book freight.

Our aim is to address questions while action can still be taken in China. Counting goods, replacing packaging or obtaining missing documents is often more practical before the shipment leaves than after it reaches your country.

## 01 | Before ordering: explain what you need the goods to do

Send the product link, specifications, intended use, quantity, destination and required timing. Tell us whether your concern is supplier identity, quality, delivery or total cost. We can help organise the questions to put to the seller before your requirements become an order.

You do not need to know every logistics term. Certification, technical, legal and destination-entry questions may require qualified specialists; we will identify where further review is needed rather than treat general guidance as a specialist opinion.

## 02 | Before payment: define the supplier review

Check the company details, available registration documents, contract and payment entity. Establish whether you are dealing with a manufacturer or trader, where the goods are made or held, and what delivery and after-sales terms apply. Resolve unexplained differences between entities before paying.

TengYoda can help coordinate document checks, supplier communication and, where agreed, site visits or independent audits. These are different services with different scopes. A review can help identify concerns, but it cannot guarantee future performance. The purchase and payment decisions remain yours.

## 03 | Confirming the order: make your requirements checkable

Record the model, material, dimensions, quantities, accessories, packaging, labels and acceptance criteria. Similar model names do not necessarily mean identical specifications. For machinery, also address operating tests, manuals and installation support.

We can help organise the supplier confirmation list and follow up on documents and timing. You and suitable technical advisers should confirm the product specifications and acceptance standards.

## 04 | Before dispatch: distinguish receiving checks from inspection

Counting cartons does not confirm every item inside or prove product performance. Receiving checks may cover carton counts, shipping marks, visible packaging condition and photos. Opening cartons, checking individual SKUs, sampling, functional testing and specialist quality inspection require an agreed scope.

TengYoda can carry out agreed warehouse checks and coordinate appropriate inspection or testing services. Define the standards, sample size, responsible party and reporting requirements before authorising shipment.

## 05 | Multiple suppliers: connect each carton to its order

Different readiness dates, unmarked cartons and partial deliveries can make consolidation confusing. Create supplier and order references, expected quantities and arrival dates, then give each seller consistent warehouse and shipping-mark instructions.

TengYoda can coordinate supplier deliveries or collection within China, track arrivals and match goods to orders. Whether to wait for everything or send urgent items first should reflect storage terms, delivery needs and actual transport costs.

## 06 | At our warehouse: our own team and documented handovers

TengYoda operates its own dedicated warehouse. Our own employees handle the receiving, dispatch and related warehouse tasks entrusted to us. Buyers can speak directly with our team about arrival records, photos and outstanding issues.

Inbound and outbound handling is monitored by CCTV. Cargo-related footage or records can be provided as agreed, alongside receiving records and photographs. Agree availability, scope and access arrangements in advance. This does not mean unrestricted remote login and does not replace a physical goods inspection.

## 07 | When an issue appears: resolve the next step before shipping

Shortages, incorrect goods, damaged packaging, missing accessories or inconsistent documents can become much harder and more expensive to address after arrival overseas. Identifying them while the supplier and goods are still in China can create more practical options.

Within the agreed scope, TengYoda can document discrepancies, contact suppliers and coordinate replacement goods, additional items, repacking or document corrections. You decide whether to accept, return, repair or release the goods. We cannot promise to resolve every dispute, but we can help avoid shipping an issue that has not been understood.

## Why the time before export matters

Consider this illustrative scenario, not a completed customer case: a small importer buys from three suppliers. One delivery has an unexpected carton count, another is delayed, and a third contains fragile goods with unsuitable packaging. Sending everything onward without review can leave the buyer discovering the issues overseas.

- **Record the discrepancy:** compare receiving records with supplier dispatch information and photograph visible concerns. Carton counts do not establish the contents of every carton.

- **Clarify the cause:** check for partial deliveries, missing goods or packing changes. Obtain authorisation and define the scope before opening cartons.

- **Present options:** compare waiting for replacements, sending urgent goods first or repacking, including cost and timing implications.

- **Follow your decision:** act on your instructions, update the cargo information and confirm release after the agreed work is complete.
TengYoda helps turn uncertainty into **documented issues, practical options and accountable follow-up**, so you can decide before the goods leave China.

## 08 | Preparing cargo: review packing, cargo details and documents together

Small items need identification and suitable consolidation; fragile goods need protection. Machinery and oversized items need handling and delivery planning. Disclose batteries, liquids, powders and chemicals before sending them to the warehouse so the required information and carrier acceptance can be reviewed.

TengYoda can coordinate packing, consolidation, shipment information and transport planning. Unconfirmed cargo or incomplete documents should be reviewed before dispatch. Vague descriptions or undisclosed cargo characteristics are not a substitute for proper preparation.

## 09 | Choosing transport: compare the full service scope

A low freight rate does not always mean a low total cost. Compare collection, warehouse handling, transport, destination charges, clearance arrangements, taxes and delivery on a consistent basis. Confirm inclusions, exclusions, dimensions, weight and quotation conditions.

TengYoda can compare courier, air, sea and split air-and-sea options against your actual cargo and timing. For door delivery, also clarify the importer, taxes, delivery address, appointments and unloading responsibilities. The agreed written scope governs the service.

## 10 | Shipment and delivery: know who owns the next action

After departure, documents, release arrangements and destination handovers still require attention. Establish who receives notices, provides missing information, approves additional costs and receives or unloads the cargo.

TengYoda can follow shipment milestones and coordinate with destination partners within the agreed scope. Our value starts before the freight booking: helping you organise the goods, information and responsibilities while action can still be taken in China.

## Questions before you contact us

### Can I ask before placing an order?

Yes. A product link, estimated quantity and destination are enough to start a conversation. Early questions can help clarify the order requirements before payment.

### I have already paid and the supplier is delaying. Can you help?

Tell us the situation so we can assess possible communication or verification support. We cannot promise recovery of funds, force a refund or resolve every dispute. Keep your order, payment and communication records, and monitor the dispute deadlines under your platform or contract. Do not let informal coordination replace time-sensitive remedies.

### Does free consultation include a factory visit or goods inspection?

No. Free consultation helps clarify the problem and possible next steps. Visits, audits, inspections, testing, collection and storage require a separately agreed scope, feasibility review and charges before you commission the work.

### Can you guarantee a supplier or shipment is risk-free?

No. Checks and documentation can help reveal problems, but findings depend on the scope, information available and actual goods. We distinguish confirmed facts, unresolved questions and decisions that need your approval.

### Can you handle every matter in China?

You are welcome to ask about uncertainties relating to sourcing and cargo movement. We first establish what we can undertake. Technical, legal, certification or other specialist work may require an appropriate provider and a separate agreement.

## You do not need a perfect quote request to get started

- The supplier name, city and whether you have ordered or paid;

- The destination, delivery location and preferred arrival time;

- Your main concern and the checks or coordination you want help with.
