Finding a supplier and agreeing a purchase price is only part of importing nail products from China. A factory may manufacture the goods without being able to complete the export arrangements. A door-to-door service may deliver the cargo without providing import records in the buyer’s own company name.

In an enquiry involving nail products for South America, TengYoda helped a buyer work through cargo documents, importer arrangements and the scope of freight charges before choosing a shipping plan.

Customer identity, quantities, specific routing and quoted prices have been removed. This is an enquiry and planning case, not a report of a completed shipment.

## Start with the products and the buyer’s requirements

The proposed order included nail liquids, powders and accessories. Some liquids required dangerous-goods review; other products needed separate verification. Small bottles and similar end uses do not establish the same transport classification.

The supplier could provide basic product and transaction documents but could not independently complete all export arrangements. The buyer also needed to import under its own company name and obtain corresponding formal import declarations and tax-payment records.

Before comparing rates, three questions therefore needed answers: can the proposed service accept the goods, who will handle export and import, and which documents will the buyer receive?

## Match the shipping service to the importer requirement

The initial door-delivery channel used a designated importer and could not meet this buyer’s requirement for import and tax records in its own company name. The parties therefore explored an LCL sea-freight arrangement with destination clearance arranged by the buyer.

**This was a limitation of the specific channel discussed, not a statement that all DDP services work the same way.** TengYoda helped identify the mismatch before dispatch, when the buyer still had the opportunity to choose another arrangement.

The bill-of-lading consignee, actual importer and recipient of import records should each be confirmed. The buyer’s destination broker should review the company’s import eligibility and product requirements before booking.

## Compare the full shipping scope, not just the ocean rate

Buyers need to understand the full budget, not just a headline freight rate. Specific customer prices are not disclosed here. A shipment-specific quotation should explain the following categories:

| Cost category | What the quotation should define |
| --- | --- |
| China pickup and transfers | Start and end points; loading, unloading, waiting and intercity transport inclusions. |
| Documents and export handling | Export-entity arrangements, declaration services and the precise document scope. |
| Dangerous-goods services | Required review, testing, documentation or packaging; provider and payer. |
| International freight | Transport mode, charging basis, surcharges and validity conditions. |
| Destination and import | Local handling, minimum charges, clearance, taxes, responsible parties and records. |
| Final delivery | Address, vehicle, appointment and unloading arrangements. |
| Additional or exceptional costs | Triggers, supporting basis, notification and approval process. |

Services may use chargeable weight, revenue tons or fixed charges. Compare the same cargo and delivery scope: a port-to-port rate is not directly comparable to a door-delivery total.

If an order changes, updated packing details are needed before recalculating. Costs should not simply be scaled by item count. Optional additional work should be priced and approved before execution, with emergency authorisation agreed in advance.

## Three questions buyers often ask

### Can I send everything to a warehouse and deal with the documents later?

Send product and packaging information first, then confirm the receiving location and acceptance conditions before dispatch. Potentially dangerous goods cannot be assumed acceptable at any consolidation warehouse.

### Can I use TengYoda only for the China-side services?

Explain which stages you want to delegate so TengYoda can confirm the available scope. This might include supplier coordination, pickup, export documents and international transport, while your own agent handles the destination. Agree the cargo and document handover points in advance.

### Does asking for advice commit me to booking freight?

Initial consultation helps clarify available documents, differences between options and outstanding questions, without a consultation fee. Testing, documentation, handling and transport services still require a separately confirmed scope and quotation.
